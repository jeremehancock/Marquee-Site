Marquee is a self-hosted web app for managing the posters in your Plex library — Movies, TV Shows, Seasons, and Collections. Import them all, refine each one in place, and send your choice back to Plex locked so it sticks.

Features

Everything you need to curate your artwork

Marquee treats Plex as the source of truth and gives you a fast, focused place to fix the posters that never looked right.

Import from Plex

Pull the current poster for every Movie, TV Show, Season, and Collection. A step-by-step picker asks what you want first, then shows only the libraries that can provide it.

Edit posters in place

Upload your own art, paste an image URL, or pick a replacement from Find Posters, the built-in online poster search.

Locked in Plex

Changing a poster uploads it to Plex and locks the artwork, so an agent refresh won't quietly replace the image you picked.

Auto-import on a schedule

Re-import every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours. Marquee skips posters that haven't changed in Plex, so recurring imports stay cheap and easy on your server.

Browse and sort your way

Filter by type or see the whole library in one grid, each poster tagged. Sort alphabetically or by date added to Plex, newest first.

Mobile-first, installable

On a phone the gallery stays front and center: navigation and poster actions live in full-size trays. Install it as a PWA on your phone or desktop.

Poster Wall

Turn a spare screen into a live now-playing board

The Poster Wall is a full-screen, slideshow view of your library that switches to whatever your household is actually watching.

  • Cycles through your posters in a clean, full-bleed slideshow
  • Shows the poster of whatever is streaming, with a Currently Streaming banner, media details, and the Plex user
  • Rotates through multiple active streams automatically
  • No sign-in required — point a spare monitor straight at it

How it works

Three steps to a better looking library

  1. 1

    Connect and import

    Point Marquee at your Plex server, choose what to import, and pick the libraries. Marquee pulls the current poster for each item and remembers what it belongs to.

  2. 2

    Refine the ones that bug you

    Hover a poster — or tap it on mobile — to open its actions. Upload a file, paste a URL, or search Find Posters, then apply it.

  3. 3

    Send it to Plex and keep it

    Marquee uploads the poster to Plex and locks the artwork. Fetch from Plex any time to pull an item's current art back, and use Orphans to tidy up what's gone.

Run it on your own server

Marquee is a single self-hosted container that talks to your Plex server — no accounts, no cloud, nothing leaves your network. Full setup instructions, configuration options, and release notes live on GitHub.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Marquee change my Plex library?

Yes. When you change a poster — or use Send to Plex — Marquee uploads that image to the item in Plex and locks the artwork so Plex keeps it.

Is Marquee a backup of my Plex posters?

No. Marquee treats Plex as the source of truth: importing pulls the current poster from Plex and overwrites what Marquee had for that item. Keep your own backups of anything you can't recreate.

What is orphan detection?

It finds posters in Marquee that are no longer linked to any media in Plex — usually because you removed that content.

Where do "Find Posters" results come from?

From online poster sources — TVDB, TMDB, Fanart.tv, and Mediux.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Marquee is responsive and installable as a PWA. On a phone, secondary navigation lives behind a menu and tapping a poster opens a full-size action sheet, so the gallery stays front and center.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Marquee is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and no telemetry.

How do I install it?

Marquee runs as a Docker container. The GitHub repository has the compose file, every configuration option, and instructions for finding your Plex token.
